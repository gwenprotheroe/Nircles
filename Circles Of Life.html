Guide

This is a demonstration of alternative visualisation technique for the tree of life. Other examples include branching trees, tree maps, and sunburst diagrams. These nested circles can be used to represent any arbitrarily complex hierarchy structure.

  • Larger circles or nested circles represent kingdoms, phylum, class, order, family or genus.
  • Smaller, innermost circles represent individual species.
